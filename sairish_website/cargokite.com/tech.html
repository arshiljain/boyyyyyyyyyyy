High-
performance design

Crafted from the ground up, our vessel architecture is entirely proprietary—engineered to harness the full potential of kite propulsion. Drawing from the precision and agility of high-performance sailing, we translate elite maritime design principles into scalable solutions for commercial shipping.

But it's more than just a ship—it's a platform. Designed with adaptability at its core, it's ready to accommodate larger kite systems, integrate advanced autonomy software, and transition to alternative fuels—all without major structural modifications. This is performance built to evolve.

24/7 Global Mobilization & Support

Our vessel is built for autonomy from the keel up. At its core is our proprietary kite controller, complemented by a modular, scalable software architecture that enables real-time decision-making, smooth coordination across onboard systems, and secure data handling throughout the vessel's digital backbone.

Seamless hardware integration ensures that sensors, actuators, and embedded electronics operate as a unified system—translating data into action with precision.

Comprehensive Fleet Experience

Our vessel class breaks with convention. Instead of scaling up, we scale smart—with ships sized at < 1000 standard containers. This shift enables a distributed fleet model where multiple agile vessels replace a single mega-ship. The result: more frequent port calls, faster point-to-point transport, and flexible capacity allocation across trades to match seasonal demand.

Behind the scenes, our coordination algorithm takes orders—each with its own origin, destination, and time window—and dynamically assigns them across the fleet. It's logistics reimagined: responsive, efficient, and built for a world that demands flexibility.
