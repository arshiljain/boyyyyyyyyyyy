OUR PURPOSE & PHILOSOPHY

Right People • Right Vessel • Right Time.

Sairish Maritime Solutions was established in Singapore with a single unwavering objective: to provide international shipowners and managers with reliable, high-calibre maritime crew who embody the highest standards of safety, professionalism, and MLC 2006 compliance.

In an industry where a single unqualified crew member or missed port relief can cause severe operational downtime and Port State Control penalties, Sairish acts as an indispensable strategic partner. We combine deep seafaring heritage with modern digital crew logistics, delivering seamless end-to-end manning across deep-sea and offshore fleets worldwide.

OUR JOURNEY & MILESTONES

Key Turning Points & Achievements

From our founding in Singapore to our expanding presence across major maritime corridors, our journey is defined by innovation, trust, and relentless service excellence.

2022 Inception & Singapore HQ

Sairish Maritime Solutions is founded in Singapore by seasoned maritime executives and technologists, dedicated to creating a transparent, 24/7 responsive crewing agency for the global commercial fleet.

2023 MLC 2006 & ISO 9001 Certification

Achieved full international SRPS (Seafarer Recruitment & Placement Service) accreditation from top-tier classification societies and established our primary deep-sea officer manning hub in Mumbai.

2024 Launch of NavisCrew Platform

Deployed our proprietary cloud-based crewing ecosystem, providing ship managers with live vessel crew matrices, digital credential verification, and automated flag-state endorsement tracking.

2025 Global Fleet Scaling (150+ Vessels)

Expanded operations into Rotterdam and Dubai, surpassing 5,000 certified seafarers deployed across 150+ international commercial vessels while maintaining an unblemished zero PSC detention record.

2026+ Decarbonized Maritime Manning

Pioneering specialized training pipelines for next-generation dual-fuel vessels (LNG, Methanol, Ammonia) and wind-assisted propulsion systems, equipping seafarers for the future of maritime trade.
