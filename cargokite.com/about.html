Introduction

People are at the heart of global shipping.

Right People • Right Time • Right Solution.

Sairish Maritime Solutions is a maritime hard tech company based in Singapore. The company was founded in 2022 with the mission of sustainable and at the same time economically viable commercial shipping.

With a strong understanding of international maritime operations, we connect shipowners and ship managers with competent, certified and experienced crew who meet high standards of professionalism, safety and MLC 2006 compliance.
